The shortest distance between two points is a straight line. But the most interesting journey? That's a zigzag.

---

Life rarely follows a linear path. We stumble, pivot, backtrack, and discover unexpected detours. Each turn reveals something we couldn't have seen from the main road.

In code, we refactor. In thinking, we iterate. In growth, we spiral upward — sometimes circling back to where we started, but always a little higher.

This is a space for those winding thoughts — technical insights, random explorations, and the occasional dead end that turned out to be a shortcut.

*Keep zigzagging.*
